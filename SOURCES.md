# Sources & Frameworks

This pack isn't generic AI advice, every skill encodes frameworks from credible,
named practitioners and primary sources. Here's the lineage.

## Built on the way Odylic Media actually runs accounts
Peter Quadrel (founder, Odylic Media) scales premium/luxury 7-8-figure DTC brands
"at the intersection of finance and AI-powered advertising," playing the role of
CMO, CFO, and Chief Data Scientist at once. Core operating beliefs baked into these skills:
- **"The algorithm doesn't care about your campaign structure… all Meta really cares about is how much people like your creative."** Creative is the primary lever.
- **"A new Meta ad account is not the place for sophistication."** Radical simplicity first, one campaign, one CBO ad set, all attribution windows on; don't restructure until ~50 weekly conversions.
- Creative as an **investment thesis**: track 20+ variables (angle, persona, hook, format, production style, offer); launch 10-15 variations, not 2-3.
- **Forecast and measure incrementally**, platform ROAS is a directional signal, not the P&L.
(Sources: [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/peter-quadrel/) · [2026 Meta Account Setup Guide](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/2026-new-meta-ad-account-setup-guide-peter-quadrel-4phoc) · [odylicmedia.com](https://www.odylicmedia.com/) · [DTC Times](https://the.dtctimes.com/p/issue-17-creative-testing-showdown))
