# Video / UGC brief and script template

On Meta the whole game is the first 3 seconds, most viewers are gone before second
3, so the hook is not part of the brief, it is the brief. Write something a creator
or an AI video tool can execute scene by scene.

## Peter's script template (use this exact shape)
```
CONCEPT: [one line]
FORMAT: [UGC / Brand / Testimonial / Comparison]
LENGTH: [XX seconds]
PERSONA: [who this targets]

[0:00-0:03] HOOK
 Visual: [what we see]
 Audio/VO: "[natural spoken line, not written copy]"
 Text Overlay: "[if any]"
[0:03-0:15] BODY
 Visual / Audio-VO / Text Overlay
[0:15-0:25] PROOF / TESTIMONIAL
 Visual / Audio-VO / Text Overlay
[0:25-0:30] CTA + End Card
 [logo + CTA]

NOTES FOR CREATOR:
 - tone / pacing / energy
 - product details to emphasize
 - what to avoid
```

## The anatomy and the rules
Three decisions stacked. Hook (0 to 3s) decides if they watch. Body (3 to 25s)
decides if they believe, deliver the angle. CTA (25s+) decides if they act. Earn
each zone in order, you cannot fix a bad hook with a great body.
- Hooks must work without audio, always give a text-overlay backup.
- Write dialogue the way people actually speak, contractions and natural filler, a 3-second attention window.
- Specify whether the script is for in-house, an influencer, or a UGC creator.
- 2 to 3 alternate hooks to test, this is the highest-leverage variation, it is what the media buyer tests.

## Text-overlay conventions
Hook overlay 3 to 7 words, supporting text 8 to 15 words, CTA 2 to 4 words.
Scannability over complete sentences.

## Start from what hooks, not the product
Build on a winning angle or hook flagged by the creative analyst, name the Cascade
level. A video that visually answers a recurring objection or question from the
comment analyst is a strong concept. Voice rules apply to spoken lines and overlays:
no em dashes, natural speech, short.

## Hand-off to production
Pass the scene list and shot descriptions to higgsfield-generate (Seedance or Kling
for video, Marketing Studio for avatar or UGC, chain higgsfield-soul-id for a
consistent presenter). Pre-screen the hook with higgsfield-generate Virality
Predictor. Convert the brief into prompts, do not make the user restate it.
