# Report templates

A great report leads with the business outcome, then diagnoses. Never a metric dump.
Peter steers by the new-vs-returning split, so frame platform ROAS as directional
and the new-customer numbers as the truth.

## The rule that governs every number
Platform-reported ROAS overstates by 2 to 3x (view-through, double-counting, iOS).
So NCMER, NCRPM, nCAC, and contribution margin are the truth, platform ROAS is a
directional signal. Never hand a client a platform ROAS as if it is the P&L.

## Daily report (house style)
Forecast vs actual, prose, not a dashboard. Shape:
```
[Day] | [Date] | Channel Performance Notes

Meta Spend: Forecast / Actual
Meta NC ROAS: Forecast / Actual

[1 to 2 short paragraphs on Meta: spend, campaign trading, efficiency, CPMs.]
[1 paragraph on Google: brand vs non-brand, statics, YouTube.]
[Optional one-line closer on any other channel.]
```
Style rules, follow them exactly:
- Prose only. No bold, no headers inside the body.
- Comma splices are fine. "Though" as a connector is on-brand.
- Short, dense, plain. Abbreviations are expected: camps., DoD, NC ROAS, aMER, TOF, RC, NB, YT.
- The user usually fills the metric numbers, you write the prose around them. Ask for the numbers if they are not provided, do not invent them.

## Weekly report (client-ready)
```
[Brand], Weekly, [date range]

HEADLINE, 1 to 2 sentences, the story of the week
SCORECARD, this week vs last with deltas: spend, new-customer revenue, NCMER, nCAC / new customers, AOV, contribution margin if known. Platform ROAS as a labeled diagnostic line.
WHAT MOVED AND WHY, 2 to 4 plain-English drivers
CREATIVE NOTES, top and bottom by spend and revenue, fatigue flags, diversity gaps
DANGER ZONE, anything below break-even or with negative marginal contribution margin
WHAT WE DID / WHAT IS NEXT, actions taken, the plan, specific, owned, dated
```

## Tone and channel
Slack, lead with an emoji status (green on track, yellow watch, red problem), tight
bullets. Email or doc, a touch more formal. Match the audience, a founder wants
new-customer efficiency and the plan, a marketing lead may want creative detail. Be
honest about a bad week and name the fix.

## Daily forecasting feeds the report
The report is only as good as the forecast behind it. Worth tracking daily:
projected spend by channel, new vs returning revenue targets, purchase volume,
contribution margin or profit goal, CAC thresholds.
