# Static / carousel brief

Fill this completely. A good brief turns a winning pattern into something a designer
or an image model can execute without a meeting. Ground it in current account winners.

## What "done" looks like (Peter's static brief contents)
- **CONCEPT**, one line.
- **ANGLE**, the single argument. Name the Cascade level you are working (segment, persona, or angle).
- **HOOK**, the focal visual plus the headline that lands in under a second.
- **LAYOUT with copy zones**, top text, on-image callouts, product placement, logo, CTA, top to bottom. Minimal text, legible at thumbnail size.
- **VISUAL DIRECTION**, style, mood, color, lighting, props, model or no model, background, 2 to 3 references.
- **COPY ON THE CREATIVE**, exact words to set in the image, short, high contrast.
- **SPECS**, aspect ratio and format.
- **The UCID**, the cross-platform creative id, so the asset is trackable across Meta, Google, and AppsFlyer.
- **CAPTION (post copy)**, primary text, headline, CTA button, hand to the copywriter.
- **VARIATIONS**, 2 to 4 test variants (different hook image, headline, or background) so it is a test, not a one-off.

## Text-overlay conventions
Hook overlay 3 to 7 words, supporting text 8 to 15 words, CTA 2 to 4 words.
Scannability over complete sentences.

## What makes a static work
Design for sound-off, thumb-speed, small screen. One big focal point, few words,
high contrast. The hook on a static is the focal image plus the headline read in
under a second. A static that answers the top objection or FAQ from the comment
analyst head-on is often a fast winner. Tie the concept to a proven angle from the
creative analyst, new execution, proven argument.

## Format cheat-sheet
Feed 1:1 (1080x1080) or 4:5 (1080x1350), 4:5 takes more screen and is usually
preferred. Stories and Reels 9:16 (1080x1920), keep key content in the center safe
zone. Carousel 1:1 cards, brief each card's role (hook, benefit, proof, offer, CTA).

## Hand-off to production
Pass the visual direction and copy zones to an image generator (higgsfield-product-photoshoot
for product, lifestyle, or ad-creative, higgsfield-generate GPT Image 2 for
text-on-image). Translate the brief into the prompt, do not make the user re-type it.
