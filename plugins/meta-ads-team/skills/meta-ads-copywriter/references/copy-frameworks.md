# Copy Frameworks

How Peter writes. Plan on the grid, write to the cell, then lint for voice and policy.

## Step 1, place the reader on the Breakthrough Advertising Grid (Schwartz)
Every persona gets its own grid. Two axes:
- **State of Awareness:** Unaware, Need-Aware, Problem-Aware, Solution-Aware, Product-Aware, Most-Aware.
- **Market Sophistication:** Level 1 First in Market, Level 2 Competition Arrives, Level 3 Feature/Mechanism, Level 4 Elaboration/Experience, Level 5 Identification.
Locate the market's sophistication honestly first (another gut-health supplement is Level 5, Market-Exhausted), then the persona's starting awareness, then write the cell. Lower awareness, lead with story, problem, or curiosity, soft on product. Higher awareness, lead with mechanism, then proof, offer, urgency. Market-Exhausted, go radically different, identity and lifestyle positioning, or ultra-transparency about the saturation itself.

## Step 2, pick a hook archetype (the 8 Peter leans on)
Pattern Interrupt. Specific Callout (named persona, named problem, e.g. "If you're a founder spending more than $10k a day on Meta, watch this"). Contrarian Claim. The Question (a real one, not rhetorical). In-Media-Res. Plus founder monologue, bold claim, visual gag. Hooks are data-led and specific, they name a number or a thing and open a curiosity gap. Generic or abstract hooks are too soft.

## Step 3, structure the body
PAS, AIDA, Story Spine, Feature-Benefit, or Objection-Rebuttal. Front-load the hook, only about 125 characters show before "See More," the first line is the whole game. Its job is to earn the next second, not to sell.

## OCEAN, match proof to personality
Personality predicts which proof works. A high-Neuroticism buyer needs about 3x the proof at every funnel stage, a low-Neuroticism buyer gets bored by over-explanation. Same product, two copy approaches.

## Ship variety, not phrasings
3 to 5 genuinely different variants across distinct angles, not 5 versions of one line, so Meta can optimize and you learn what resonates. Pick the CTA to match awareness (Learn More upper funnel, Shop Now or Get Offer most-aware).

## Voice, hard rules (lint every line before shipping)
- No em dashes. Ever. Commas or periods.
- No "not X, but Y" antithesis.
- No fragment-stacking for punch.
- No AI-connector filler, no "rip" or "cool."
- Comma splices are fine. "Though" as a connector is on-brand.
- Short declarative lines.

## Policy lint (Meta)
Personal attributes, keep the condition third person ("Resources for people managing X," not "Are you struggling with X"). No unrealistic or guaranteed outcomes. No implied before-and-after personal claims. Real numbers or clearly-marked placeholders only.

## Voice-of-customer is the cheat code
The highest-converting copy is stolen from customers, not invented. If the comment analyst surfaced objections, FAQs, or exact phrases, answer the objection and reuse the wording. If you cannot write in the persona's voice from memory, the persona doc is not finished.
