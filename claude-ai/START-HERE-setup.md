# Add the Meta Ads Team to your Claude

9 AI specialists that run your Meta ads: a data analyst, a creative analyst, a
comment analyst, a media buyer, a copywriter, two creative briefers, a reporter,
and a team lead that routes it all. Setup takes about 5 minutes.

## What you need
A Claude Pro or Max account (claude.ai). Skills and connectors need a paid plan.

## Step 1, turn on Skills
In Claude, open Settings, then Capabilities. Turn on Code execution (and file
creation if it is shown). This lets Claude run skills.

## Step 2, connect your Meta ad account
1. Get a free token at pipeboard.co/api-tokens.
2. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
3. Paste this URL with your token on the end:
   https://meta-ads.mcp.pipeboard.co/?token=YOUR_TOKEN_HERE
4. Save and finish any login prompt. You will test it in Step 4.

## Step 3, add the 9 skills
In Claude, open Customize, then Skills, then Create skill, then Upload a skill. Pick
one zip from the individual-skills folder, then turn it on. Repeat for all 9.
The team lead is meta-ads-team. The other eight are the specialists.

## Step 4, use it
Talk to your account in plain English. Try:
- "List my Meta ad accounts."
- "How did the account do last week, and what should I change?"
- "Which creatives are winning, and what angle should we make more of?"
- "Write me 5 hooks for the new product."
- "Scale the winners and pause anything below break-even." (the buyer asks first)
- "Brief a UGC video from our best-performing angle."
- "Send me the weekly recap."

## Good to know
The media buyer can spend real money. It always proposes the changes and waits for
your yes before it does anything. Nothing happens to your account without approval.
